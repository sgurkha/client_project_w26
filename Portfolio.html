Portfolio Support

Design a Portfolio that helps you display what you can bring to the table!

Your portfolio is often how recruiters see whether you are a good fit for the role. It is an opportunity for you to highlight your unique strengths, skills, and experiences to potential employers. Whether you’re starting from scratch or looking to polish an existing portfolio, these resources are here to help you every step of the way.

UMSI students are in high demand, and a clear, well-organized portfolio will help you stand out among applicants. On this page, you'll find practical tips, templates, and guides—all tailored to the needs of School of Information students and aligned with employer expectations.

Need feedback? You can always meet with a CDO Career Coach to review your materials.
