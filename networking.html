Connect and learn how to network!

Ready to take the next step toward your future career? The UMSI Career Development Resource Hub is your one-stop shop for trusted guidance, practical tools, and the latest information to help you thrive as a student and job-seeker. Whether you are just starting your exploration or getting ready to launch your career, you will find everything you need, organized for you.

Your networking skill is often how recruiters are able to get connected to you to see whether you are a good fit for the role. It is an opportunity for you to highlight your unique strengths, skills, and experiences to potential employers. Whether you're starting from scratch or looking to polish an existing portfolio, these resources are here to help you every step of the way.

UMSI students are in high demand, and a clear, well-organized portfolio will help you stand out among applicants. On this page, you'll find practical tips, templates, and guides—all tailored to the needs of School of Information students and aligned with employer expectations.

Need feedback? You can always meet with a CDO Career Coach to review your materials.
