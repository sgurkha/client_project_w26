Visit the School of Information

The University of Michigan School of Information is located in beautiful Ann Arbor, Michigan, on the U-M North Campus. Whether you're visiting for an appointment, attending an event, or just exploring our facilities, this guide will help you find your way.

Leinweber Computer Science and Information Building

📍 Address

Leinweber Computer Science and Information Building
2200 Hayward Street
Ann Arbor, MI 48109-2121

About the Building

The Leinweber Building is the home of the University of Michigan School of Information, housing the school's operations and graduate programs. This state-of-the-art complex opened in May 2025 and features:

  • Modern classrooms designed for collaborative learning
  • Maker labs and design studios with cutting-edge equipment
  • Spontaneous conversation spots woven throughout the building
  • Space designed for bold ideas and interdisciplinary collaboration

Need detailed navigation? Download the UMSI Walking and Parking Map for more detailed information about the building and surrounding areas.

Accessibility Information

The Leinweber Building is fully accessible. Here are the best entrances depending on your needs:

♿ Ramp Access

South Entrance (Most Accessible)

  • First floor entry
  • Direct accessible ramp access from parking lot NC27
  • Elevators located down the main hall
  • Recommended: This is the most accessible route

East Entrance (Alternative)

  • Third floor entry
  • Ramp access from parking lot NC48
  • Accessible, but note: lengthy ramp path from back of lot

🛗 Elevator Access

South Entrance (First Floor)

  • Continue straight from the entrance
  • Elevators will be on the left

East Entrance (Third Floor)

  • Turn left from the entrance
  • Elevators will be on your right

Parking

Visitor Parking - Lot NC27 (Recommended)

  • Location: Just south of the Leinweber Building
  • Access: Enter from Murfin Avenue, just south of Hayward Street
  • Building Entry: South entrance, first level
  • Elevators: Located down the main hall
  • Accessibility: ✅ Most accessible option with direct ramp access
  • Payment: Card-only payment using the app AimsMobilePay.com

Additional Visitor Parking - Lot NC48

  • Location: Adjacent to the Leinweber Building on the north
  • Building Entry: Third level (main entrance to the school)
  • Accessibility: ✅ Accessible, but lengthy ramp path from back of lot
  • Payment: Card-only payment using the app AimsMobilePay.com

💡 Pro Tip: Download the AimsMobilePay app before your visit to streamline parking payment.

Getting to UMSI from Detroit Metro Airport

Airport Options

The Detroit Metropolitan Wayne County Airport (DTW) is the major international airport closest to Ann Arbor, located about 35 miles away.

Other nearby airports include:

  • Toledo Express Airport in Toledo, Ohio
  • Bishop International Airport in Flint, Michigan

Directions from DTW to UMSI

  1. Travel west on I-94 from the airport
  2. Exit onto US-23 north
  3. Exit and turn west toward Ann Arbor on Plymouth Road
  4. Turn left at a traffic light onto Murfin Avenue
  5. Turn left on Hayward Street
  6. Turn right into parking lot NC48, adjacent to the Leinweber Building on the north
  7. Enter the building — you will be on the third level (main entrance to the school)

Estimated driving time: 45-60 minutes depending on traffic

Building Layout & Spaces

The Leinweber Building spans five floors, each with different facilities and services. Here's what you'll find on each level:

🎓 Level 1 - Learning & Making

  • Classrooms with state-of-the-art technology
  • Maker lab with equipment for hands-on learning
  • Design studio for collaborative projects
  • Access to parking lot NC27 through south entrance

📚 Level 2 - Student Resources

  • Classrooms and lecture halls
  • Student lounge for collaboration and relaxation
  • Admissions office
  • Student affairs
  • Career services & the Career Development Office ✅
  • Shared user study room for research and testing

🎯 Level 3 - Main Entrance & Administration

  • Main entrance to the school (from parking lot NC48)
  • Dean's Suite
  • Administrative offices
  • Founder's Room
  • Engaged learning office
  • Access to parking lot NC48 through Level 3 entrance

👨‍🔬 Level 4 - Faculty & Innovation

  • Faculty offices
  • Post-doc and PhD student offices
  • 4th floor lounge for collaboration
  • Dow Room
  • AR/VR Lab with cutting-edge immersive technology

🤝 Level 5 - Faculty & Development

  • Faculty offices
  • Post-doc and PhD student offices
  • 5th floor lounge
  • Development & Alumni Relations office

📌 Important for Career Development Visitors

The Career Development Office is located on Level 2. This is where you'll find career coaches, resources, and support for resumes, interviews, networking, and more.
